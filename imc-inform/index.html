The Mind Children have come to the village of Compton Magna to prepare Earth for colonisation. They see young people as vital to the success of this mission and seek to protect them. Your mission is to collect six pieces of evidence of wrong-doing against the young and take them to the Mind Children HQ. Tip: don't forget to examine things closely!
